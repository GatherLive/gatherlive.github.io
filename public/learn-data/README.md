# Geography pack

195 countries: the 193 UN members, the Holy See (Vatican City), and Palestine. Reviewed **26 October 2026**. Regions follow the UN regional grouping; transcontinental countries use one group for filtering.

- **Flags:** local SVGs from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons), MIT licensed, revision `ac7e0eb3259eb62aa6100c435607e2fe03896a97`.
- **Map, coordinates, and baseline capital facts:** [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), public domain. Map paths are simplified 1:110m country outlines in an equirectangular projection. Tiny states use location markers. Borders are for learning and do not imply an endorsement of territorial claims.
- **Names, accepted spellings, and capital notes:** curated factual corrections. See [LICENSES.txt](LICENSES.txt) for the flag artwork license.

All learning assets are served locally with Gather. No tile service, external image request, API key, or paid geography API is needed.

Afghanistan's image follows flag-icons' republic tricolour convention. A learning note distinguishes this from the white flag used by the de facto authorities. Flag usage and recognition can differ by context.

## Capital checks

Some countries have several capital functions or a planned relocation. Notes distinguish these; practice accepts each capital listed in `capital`, plus its spellings in `capitalAliases`.

- [Equatorial Guinea government decree](https://www.guineaecuatorialpress.com/noticias/decreto_ley_por_el_que_se_declara_la_ciudad_de_la_paz_djibloho_capital_de_la_republica_de_guinea_ecuatorial): Ciudad de la Paz replaced Malabo on 2 January 2026.
- [Jakarta government](https://m.beritajakarta.id/en/read/152669/pramono-jakarta-remains-the-capital-city-until-a-presidential-decree-is-issued): Jakarta remains the capital pending a formal transfer decree; Nusantara is the planned replacement.
- [Kazakhstan presidency](https://www.akorda.kz/public/en/republic_of_kazakhstan/kazakhstan): Astana.
- [Burundi presidency](https://presidence.gov.bi/le-pays/): Gitega is political; Bujumbura is economic.
- [South African government](https://www.gov.za/about-sa/south-africa-glance): Pretoria, Cape Town, and Bloemfontein have separate functions.
- [Sri Lankan government](https://www.gov.lk/sri-lanka/country-overview): Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte is administrative; Colombo is commercial.
- [Swiss foreign ministry](https://www.aboutswitzerland.eda.admin.ch/en/political-system): Bern is the federal city and de facto capital.
- [Palau government](https://55piflm.gov.pw/about): Ngerulmud, in Melekeok State.
- [UN country profile](https://data.un.org/CountryProfile.aspx?crName=State%20of%20Palestine): Palestine designates East Jerusalem; Jerusalem's final status remains disputed.

This is a reviewed snapshot, not an automatic live feed. Recheck the sources before changing capital answers or updating this pack.
